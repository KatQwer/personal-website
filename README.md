This is my personal portfolio that's live and shows all the newest information you need to know about me. It can be found [here](https://jeff-tielen.netlify.app/).

## Page Information
**Home:** My home page has some base information about most pages, it shows of a couple projects from my projects page, it can redirect you to any page, gives a snapshot of my skills and a little introduction about who I am.

**CV:** My CV page is simple, it displays everything that's on my actual CV while also allowing you to view and download a PDF of my CV.

**Projects:** My projects page shows off every single project that I'm proud to call my own. Whether this is school projects or personal projects.

**About Me:** My about me page gives some information about who I am as a person. It just gives a background as to who I am while keeping it all very simple for everyone to read.

**Contact** My contact page displays a simple contact form where you can contact me about inquiries, etc.

**Legal Notice:** My legal notice page just shows a small legal disclaimer about my website.
